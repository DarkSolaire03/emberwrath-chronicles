Chapter Zero · The Setting

The World of Obsidara

Once, Obsidara was a kingdom of black glass and golden spires. Then the sky broke open, the mountains bled fire, and the realm became a graveyard of thrones — a shattered obsidian land where ember storms scour the ruins and nothing buried ever truly stays dead.

The Shattered Realm

Obsidara is a land of fractured kingdoms, each one a shard of black glass from the old empire. Jagged obsidian plains, drowned cathedrals and ash-choked forests stretch between warring city-states that all claim the same dead crown.

Ember Storms

When the wind turns red, the ember storms come — burning rains of cinder that strip flesh from bone and set the very air alight. Travellers shelter in stone or not at all. The storms are said to follow Emberwrath wherever it walks.

Ruined Kingdoms

Thrones of ash, banners of soot, kings who rule over rubble. The old noble houses still trade in oaths, relics and blood, clinging to the etiquette of a court that burned centuries ago.

The Obsidian Throne

At the heart of the realm stands a throne carved from a single slab of living obsidian. It is said to choose its ruler — and to devour the ones it rejects. The Queen who sits it has not aged in a hundred years.

“Obsidara does not forgive. It remembers — in ash, in glass, and in the names of the dead.” — Saying of the Ashen Marches

The Five Who Shape the Fire

Characters

Five souls bound to the ember — saint, warlord, prince, cataclysm and queen. Tap any card to read more of their story. (Placeholder lore — the author may edit and expand each tale.)

✦

Inferna

The Ember Saint · Flame Oracle

  • Domain: Sacred Flame & Prophecy
  • Weapon / Power: The Everflame — visions read in ash and cinder

Inferna was once a nameless acolyte who walked into a burning chapel and walked out wreathed in fire that would not consume her. Now she speaks the future in riddles of smoke, and kings cross the ash to kneel before her — though every prophecy she gives costs her a memory of the life she had before the flame.

The Everflame does not burn the faithful, the priests say — but Inferna has never once confirmed it. She keeps a charred prayer book in which she writes down the names of every soul her visions could not save.

⚔

Malphur

The Ashen Warlord · Oath-Breaker Knight

  • Domain: The Ashen Marches & Broken Oaths
  • Weapon / Power: Oathrender — a greatsword that severs vows as easily as flesh

Malphur swore his oath to a king who betrayed him, so he broke it — and broke the kingdom with it. Now the Ashen Warlord marches at the head of the Oathless, a legion of deserters and the damned, and every banner he burns is a promise the old world will never get back.

He still wears the shattered crest of the house he served, hammered flat and blackened. Some say he is searching for an oath worth keeping; others say he only wants to watch the last throne of Obsidara burn like all the rest.

🔥

Baelfire

The Hellfire Prince · Cursed Fire Entity

  • Domain: The Burning Deep & Hellfire
  • Weapon / Power: Hellfire that burns the soul, not the body

Baelfire was born a prince and remade into something else — a fire with a crown, a hunger with a name. His hellfire leaves cities standing and their people hollow, and he collects the debts of the damned the way other princes collect taxes. He smiles when he offers you a bargain, because he already knows your answer.

Legends say the curse can be broken only by a soul that owes him nothing — and that in all the centuries of the Burning Deep, no such soul has ever been found.

☠

Emberwrath

The Living Cataclysm · Wrath Embodied

  • Domain: The Emberstorm itself & the Shattering
  • Weapon / Power: The Cataclysm — ancient wrath given living form

Emberwrath is not a person, not truly — it is the world's oldest anger, wearing the shape of a burning colossus. It was the fire that shattered Obsidara, the storm that never ended, and every war since has only fed it. To face Emberwrath is to face the consequence of everything the realm has ever done.

Some scholars claim Emberwrath was summoned; others that it was always there, sleeping beneath the obsidian, waiting for a world cruel enough to wake it. Either way, it is waking again — and the ember storms are only its breathing.

♛

Obsidara

The Obsidian Queen · Heart of the Realm

  • Domain: The Obsidian Throne & the Realm itself
  • Weapon / Power: The Black Glass Crown — command over obsidian and the heart of the realm

Obsidara is the realm and the realm is her: the Obsidian Queen whose throne and whose name are one and the same. She has ruled from black glass for a hundred years without aging, holding the shattered kingdoms together by will, terror and a patience as cold and sharp as the stone she wears. None remember the girl she was before the crown — least of all the Queen herself.

It is whispered that the throne is not a seat but a bargain: for as long as she rules, Obsidara endures — and the day she leaves it, the realm will finally finish shattering.

The Story So Far

Chronicles

The scattered history of Obsidara, told in chapters. (Placeholder chapters — new entries will be added as the story is written.)

I. Ashes of the First Throne

In the last days of the old empire, a young queen takes a crown of black glass — and the sky answers with fire. The Shattering begins, and Obsidara is broken in a single burning night.

“The night the throne was crowned, the stars went out one by one, like candles pinched between a god's fingers.”— Opening line

II. The Oath That Broke the Sky

Malphur, the realm's greatest knight, discovers his king's betrayal on the eve of battle — and makes a choice that will damn a legion, unmake a kingdom, and loose the ember storms upon the Marches.

“He did not raise his sword against the king that day. He raised it against the oath itself — and the sky broke with the sound of it.”— Opening line

III. The Queen of Black Glass

A century after the Shattering, Inferna's prophecy draws a saint, a warlord and a hellfire prince toward the Obsidian Throne — while beneath the realm, something ancient begins to wake.

“The Queen had not slept in a hundred years, and the throne had not once let her forget it.”— Opening line
